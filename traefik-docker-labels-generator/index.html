Traefik Docker Labels Generator

Describe your service and get the Traefik labels for docker-compose.yml in list and map syntax, a ready service snippet, and the same routers, middlewares and service as file-provider YAML. Each label comes with a one-line explanation, and every warning names the value to fix. Built from the Traefik v3.7 and v2.11 documentation and source.
