ESP32 GPIO Pin Picker

Pick a chip and what you need the pin for, and get every GPIO ranked from safe to avoid, with the reason for each verdict: strapping pins and their boot levels, flash and PSRAM pins, USB, UART0, input-only pins, ADC1 and ADC2, touch, deep-sleep wake and DAC. Pin data comes from Espressif's datasheets and ESP-IDF, cross-checked against ESPHome's own pin validation.

!

This is chip-level data. Your dev board may already wire a pin to a BOOT button, an LED, a USB-UART bridge, a display or a sensor, and a module may not lead every pin out. Check your board's schematic before you solder.
