Home Assistant Automation Schedule Visualizer

Build an automation in the form or paste its YAML, with time, time pattern and sun triggers plus time, sun and trigger conditions. The visualizer runs ports of Home Assistant 2026.10.4's own scheduling code and of astral 2.2, the sun library it uses, lists the next runs with the trigger that fired and whether the conditions let it through, draws a seven-day timeline, and explains the tricky parts: what /5 matches, the minute and second defaults, windows that cross midnight and days when the clocks change.

        How the visualizer works
        
      
          
        
        • Ported from source. The matcher is a line-by-line port of find_next_time_expression_time from Home Assistant 2026.10.4 (homeassistant/util/dt.py), driven the way helpers/event.py drives it: after each run it looks for the next match from one second later. It passes the DST cases in Home Assistant's own tests. Validation follows triggers/time.py, triggers/time_pattern.py, sun/trigger.py, sun/condition.py and the time condition in helpers/condition.py.
          • 
        
        • Sun times. A port of astral 2.2 (the version Home Assistant pins): sunrise and sunset are the moments the sun's upper edge meets the horizon, with astral's refraction formula and the horizon dip for your elevation. The port matches the reference times in Home Assistant's own tests to the microsecond. astral's tests accept a minute of difference from published tables, and Home Assistant notes that its rise and set times get unstable where the sun only grazes the horizon near the poles.
          • 
        
        • Time of each run. Runs show the second a trigger fires. Home Assistant adds a random 0.05 to 0.5 seconds to every time and time pattern run to spread the load, which conditions with whole-second times never notice.
          • 
        
        • YAML. A small parser with PyYAML's YAML 1.1 rules, like Home Assistant's loader for files on disk: an unquoted 19:45:00 is the number 71100 and 010 is octal 8. The automation editor's YAML mode parses in your browser with a YAML 1.2 library, where such values stay text. Anchors, aliases and several documents in one paste are refused with a message.
          • 
        
        • Not simulated. Times from helpers, sensors or templates, state and event triggers and conditions, the newer sun triggers other than sunrise, sunset, dawn and dusk, automation modes, and runs missed while Home Assistant is offline. Each one the visualizer meets is listed instead of guessed.
          • 
      
        
      
        Sources: time trigger, time pattern trigger, sunrise trigger, time condition, sun condition, dt.py at 2026.10.4, astral 2.2 sun.py.
