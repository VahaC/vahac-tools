robots.txt Generator and Validator

Build a robots.txt with guided allow/disallow rules and sitemap lines, or paste an existing file to find mistakes and check which URLs a crawler may fetch. Rules are read the way RFC 9309 and Google's parser read them. Nothing leaves your browser.

Start from a preset

A preset replaces the groups below. Sitemaps and AI crawler choices stay as they are.

User-agent groups

Each group names one or more crawlers (* means every crawler without its own group) and lists the paths they may or may not crawl. The longest matching rule wins; on a tie, Allow wins.

Block AI crawlers

Checked crawlers get one shared group with Disallow: /. Search crawlers such as Googlebot and Bingbot are not affected.

robots.txt is a request, not access control: reputable crawlers honour it, others may not.

Sitemaps

Use full URLs, including https://. WordPress 5.5+ serves /wp-sitemap.xml; SEO plugins often use /sitemap_index.xml.

Your robots.txt

Upload it to the root of your site so it answers at https://your-domain/robots.txt. Each subdomain, protocol and port needs its own file.
